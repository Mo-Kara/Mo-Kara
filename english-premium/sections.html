Your learning path

You always know the next step

Built by Coach Mo. A step is done when you or your child can do it confidently – not after a fixed number of hours.

For kids: 3 stages, 18 stickers

Star Stage: Courage Explorerusually ages 3–5 · trust and safety basics
  1. Water Friend – face in the water, blowing bubbles
  2. Pool Edge Climber – holding the edge and moving along it
  3. Diver Eyes – head under, eyes open
  4. Starfish Float – floating on front and back, gliding
  5. Jump Hero – jumping in and back to the edge
  6. Exit Pro – climbing out without a ladder
Penguin Stage: Water Herousually from age 5–6 · goal 25 m
  1. Backstroke Pro – resting and swimming on the back
  2. Self-Rescuer – falling into deep water, resting, back to the edge
  3. Ring Diver – fetching a dive ring from the bottom
  4. Flutter Kick – gliding and 10 m crawl kick
  5. Breaststroke Kick – a clean frog kick
  6. Breath Rhythm – breathing out into the water regularly
  7. 25m Hero – 25 m without stopping or aids
Shark Stage: Master of the Seasafter the Penguin Stage · technique & endurance
  1. Side Breathing – front crawl breathing to the side
  2. Headfirst Dive – from the pool edge into deep water
  3. Long Distance – 200 m in max. 15 minutes
  4. Deep Diver – diving to the bottom in 2 m deep water
  5. Individual Medley – back, breast, crawl and dolphin kick

For adults: 3 paths, 6 steps each

1. Calm & confident in the wateryou feel uneasy in deep water or avoid it
  1. I can enter the pool and stand calmly in chest-deep water.
  2. I can breathe out calmly with my face in the water.
  3. I can stand up on my own from front and back position.
  4. I can float on my front and back and glide a short way.
  5. I can swim a short distance with regular strokes.
  6. I can float or tread water in deep water and get out on my own.
2. Learn front crawlyou swim breaststroke and want to learn crawl
  1. I can glide stretched out and relaxed.
  2. I can kick a steady crawl kick from the hips.
  3. I can turn my head to breathe without lifting it.
  4. I can lead the crawl arm with a high elbow.
  5. I can link arms and legs into a continuous stroke.
  6. I can swim a longer distance of crawl with regular breathing.
3. Swim better & longeryou already swim and want to get better or fitter
  1. Together we look at technique, endurance and breathing and set your goal.
  2. I can deliberately correct a chosen technique fault.
  3. I can swim a length with fewer strokes.
  4. I can swim longer distances with steady technique.
  5. I can hold a relaxed breathing rhythm over the distance.
  6. I can train independently with my own plan.

We check the starting point in the first lesson; if you already have some skills, you start further along. This is not medical treatment: with a strong fear of water, please also talk to your doctor or a psychologist.

FAQ

Good to know

Is the whole lesson really in English?

Yes. Mo teaches the full lesson in English – instructions, feedback and your questions. The booking page is in English too.

From what age can my child join?

Children can start from age 3. Adults of every age are welcome too – whether you want to overcome a fear of water, learn front crawl or improve your technique.

Why 30 or 45 minutes?

30 minutes match the focus and strength of children aged 3 to 6 – without getting cold or exhausted. From age 7 and for adults, 45 minutes are ideal to build technique and endurance.

Can two people swim together?

Yes, in the 45-minute sessions – for siblings, couples or friends. They need the same swimming level; age does not matter, so nobody is bored or overwhelmed.

Is pool entry included?

No. You pay the entry to Bad zur Sonne directly at the till.

What if we are ill?

With the Flex Pass and the Winter Pass you move sessions yourself in the app up to 24 hours before. Sunday and Intensive courses have fixed dates – missed sessions cannot be made up or refunded. With the optional illness cover (€15 for 30 min, €25 for 45 min) you can make up one session missed because of illness, with a doctor's note.

Where do the lessons take place?

At Bad zur Sonne, Feuerbachgasse 11-13, 8020 Graz – in pleasantly warm water. New from February 2027 also in Gratkorn.

New from February 2027 at Hallenbad Gratkorn: holiday group courses for kids, adult courses on Thursdays and private lessons – pool entry included.
